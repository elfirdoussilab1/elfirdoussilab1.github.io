I'm a PhD student at the [University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/) working (mostly) on **Generative Modelling** and **Reinforcement Learning**.

I completed the [MVA](https://www.master-mva.com) master's program (Mathématiques, Vision et Apprentissage) at [Institut Polytechnique de Paris](https://www.ip-paris.fr/) together with an engineering degree in applied mathematics and computer science at [Télécom Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/). I was also fortunate to have worked with amazing researchers at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/), [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/) and the [Technology Innovation Institute](https://www.tii.ae/).

I am usually open to collaborations on the topics listed below. Feel free to reach out via email if you want to work together on exciting projects.

Check my [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Noq0ExIAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao) for a list of updated publications.
