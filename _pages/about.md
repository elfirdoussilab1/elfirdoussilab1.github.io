I'm a PhD student at the [University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/) working (mostly) on **Generative Modelling** and **Reinforcement Learning**.

I completed the [MVA master's program](https://www.master-mva.com) (Mathématiques, Vision et Apprentissage) at [Institut Polytechnique de Paris](https://www.ip-paris.fr/) with an engineering degree in applied mathematics and computer science at [Télécom Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/). I was also fortunate to have worked with amazing researchers at **Google DeepMind**, **EPFL** and **Technology Innovation Institute**.
